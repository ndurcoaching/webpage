Philosophy

Built around the athlete, not the algorithm.

Our technology-driven solution removes the guesswork from coaching, while continuing to maintain a 1:1 connection between coach and athlete. Our platform simplifies the work of a coach, but never replaces it.

01

Individualized training

At Ndur, we understand that life is busy and does not always revolve around fitness. We work with athletes to fit their personal goals, schedule, and fitness level.

02

Data-informed, not data-driven

Every plan is thoroughly reviewed and personalized to the athlete. Our software can outline a plan, but only a well-informed coach can perfect a robust schedule.

03

Recovery as training

Running is just one facet of a successful training block. Recovery, nutrition, and strength training are never neglected at Ndur.

04

Long-term athlete development

Whether you are training for your first race or targeting a big PR, we want to build a connection with you! Our program is designed to develop a community of athletes.

How it works

From sign-up to start line.

1

Complete the intake form

Provide Ndur with the necessary information to connect with you and begin creating your robust training schedule. Create an account in the client portal and connect your Strava account. You can expect to hear from us within 48 hours of clicking submit.

2

Receive your plan

You will receive access to your training in the client portal, containing recommended distance, pace, structure, and mental approach. Our plans also include endurance-focused strength exercises upon request.

3

Start training

At Ndur, we can't log the miles for you. However, as you progress through your training program, we will make adjustments to best fit your needs and fitness level. As a certified Strava API application, we see your runs in real time to ensure that training is progressing as expected. Our primary focus at Ndur is to help our athletes arrive at race day injury-free.

4

Race day

Race day is a celebration of weeks, months, sometimes even years of dedicated hard work. By creating a race day plan, including pacing and fueling strategy, Ndur reduces the stress for athletes, so they can focus on running.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you're looking for? Email nic@ndur.life.

Who is Ndur Coaching for?

Ndur is not just for elite runners. Whether you are training for your first race or chasing a faster time, we build your plan around your goals, your schedule, and your current fitness level.

What happens after I submit the intake form?

You can expect to hear from us within 48 hours. You'll create an account in the client portal, connect your Strava account, and receive your personalized training plan there.

How does my coach see my training?

Once you connect Strava in the client portal, your runs sync automatically, so your plan can be adjusted based on how training is actually going.

How does billing work?

Coaching is billed monthly through Stripe, with payment due on the 1st of each month. College students get 25% off either plan.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Either party can cancel with 14 days' written notice. Partial months already in progress aren't refunded. See the Coaching Services Agreement for details.

What if I get injured or life gets busy?

Let us know. Your plan is adjusted to fit where you are. Ndur provides fitness coaching, not medical advice, so please see a physician for any injury or health concern.
