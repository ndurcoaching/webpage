Philosophy

Built around the athlete, not the algorithm.

Our technology-driven solution removes the guesswork from coaching, while continuing to maintain a 1:1 connection between athlete and client. Our platform simplifies the work of a coach, but never replaces it.

01

Individualized training

At Ndur, we understand that life is busy and does not always revolve around fitness. We work with athletes to fit their personal goals, schedule, and fitness level.

02

Data-informed, not data-driven

Every plan is thouroughly reviewed and personalized to the athlete. Our software can outline a plan, but only a well-informed coach can perfect a robust schedule.

03

Recovery as training

Running is just one facet of a successful training block. Recovery, nutrition, and strength training are never neglected at Ndur.

04

Long-term athlete development

Whether you are training for your first race or targeting a big PR, we want to build a connection with you! Our program is designed to develop a community of athletes.

How it works

From sign-up to start line.

1

Complete the intake form

Provide Ndur with the necessary information to connect with you and begin creating your robust training schedule. Create an account in the client portal and connect your Strava account. You can expect to hear from us within 48 hours of clicking submit.

2

Receive your plan

You will recieve access to your training in the client portal, containing recommended distance, pace, structure, and mental approach. Our plans also include endurance-focused strength exercises upon request.

3

Start training

At Ndur, we can't log the miles for you. However, as you progress through your training program, we will make adjustments to best fit your needs and fitness level. As a certified Strava API application, we see your runs in real time to ensure that training is progressing as expected. Our primary focus at Ndur is to help our athletes arrive at race day injury-free.

4

Race day

Race day is a celebration of weeks, months, sometimes even years of dedicated hard work. By creating a race day plan, including pacing and fueling strategy, Ndur reduces the stress for athletes, so they can focus on running.
